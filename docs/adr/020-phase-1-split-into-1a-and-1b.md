# ADR-020: Phase 1 split into 1A and 1B

- Status: Accepted (O-11, revision 3)
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Source: docs/spec/phase-0-spec.md

## Context

A usable manual product should come before the riskiest integrations.

## Decision

Phase 1A delivers the manual workflow, evidence and Skill Inventory, Home and evaluation. Phase 1B delivers Gmail, then outreach, which is committed and high priority.

## Alternatives considered

One Phase 1; outreach deferred to Phase 2.

## Consequences

Useful product earlier; a checkpoint before Gmail.
